Stress Relief

What is stress?

Stress is the body's response to challenging situations. It can be caused by work, school, family responsibilities, or changes in everyday life. A small amount of stress may help you stay alert, but ongoing stress can take a toll on your health.

When you experience stress, your body releases hormones that prepare you to respond. Over time, frequent stress can interfere with sleep, concentration, mood, and physical well-being.

Ways to manage stress

Regular physical activity, relaxation, and healthy routines can help you manage stress. Taking breaks, getting enough sleep, and making time for activities you enjoy can also make a difference.
